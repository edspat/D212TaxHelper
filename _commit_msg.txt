Group document-type dropdown into 6 optgroups (was: flat list of 14)

User feedback: "the document-type dropdown is very long. Can we
reorganize it?". A flat list of 14 options had become hard to scan,
especially since several broker types share similar prefixes (Romania
XTB / BT / Tradeville; US Fidelity / Morgan Stanley).

Restructured into 6 <optgroup> categories chosen to match how a
Romanian fiscal-resident user typically thinks about their documents:

  ANAF / Oficial Romania            - 2 items (D-212, Adeverinta venit)
  Broker Romania                    - 5 items (XTB x2, BT x2, Tradeville)
  Broker SUA                        - 4 items (Fidelity x3, MS)
  Broker extern (UE/International)  - 1 item  (Revolut)
  Formulare fiscale internationale  - 1 item  (1042-S)
  Angajator (RSU / ESPP)            - 1 item  (MSFT Stock Award)

Each group title carries a leading flag emoji or icon in the UI
(translated via i18n) so groups are scannable even when collapsed in
the native picker on Windows / macOS.

Implementation:

  1. public/index.html - rewrapped the <select> with <optgroup> tags.
     Each optgroup has data-i18n-label="import.groupXxx" for localization.

  2. public/js/i18n.js applyTranslations() - new branch that translates
     elements with data-i18n-label by setting .label (optgroup's
     translatable property; textContent doesn't apply).

  3. public/js/app.js sortDocTypeDropdown() - now sorts options inside
     each optgroup rather than flattening + sorting globally. Preserves
     the user-chosen grouping order while keeping alphabetical order
     within each group.

  4. public/locales/{ro,en}.json - 6 new keys: import.groupAnaf,
     import.groupBrokerRo, import.groupBrokerUs, import.groupBrokerForeign,
     import.groupTaxForms, import.groupEmployer.

  5. TEXT_PDF_TYPES (OCR hint heuristic) extended to include bt_dividends,
     bt_portfolio, revolut_statement - they all produce text-extractable
     PDFs, not scans.

Smoke-tested via Edge CDP: 6 optgroups render with the expected counts
(2 + 5 + 4 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 14 total options); each group's items appear
in alphabetical order.

215/216 tests pass (1 pre-existing skipped). check-i18n clean.
